features:-
1)Topic Difficulty Analysis: Use AI to estimate question difficulty levels.

2)Trend Analysis: Identify recurring concepts over different years' question papers.

3)Custom Search: Allow users to find questions based on chapters or concepts.

4)Summary Generator: Provide a brief syllabus summary for quick revision.

5)Concept Mapping: Visual representation of concepts and their relationships.

6)PDF Extraction Library Choice — Use pdfplumber (better for tables/structured syllabi) or PyPDF2 (simpler, faster)? Recommend: pdfplumber as primary with PyPDF2 fallback.

7)Gemini Model Selection — Use gemini-1.5-pro (1M token context, better for large PDFs) or gemini-1.5-flash (faster, cheaper)? Recommend: gemini-1.5-pro for accuracy.

8)Concept Map Visualization — Use streamlit-agraph (interactive graph) or plotly (simpler charts)? Recommend: streamlit-agraph for richer concept relationships.
